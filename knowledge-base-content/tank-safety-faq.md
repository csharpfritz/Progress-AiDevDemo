# Oil Tank Safety FAQ

**How do I know if my oil tank needs to be replaced?**
Look for rust, wet spots, or a soft/spongy feel on the tank's surface, an oil odor near the tank, or a fill pipe that whistles unusually during delivery. Aboveground tanks typically last 15-20 years; underground tanks are more prone to corrosion and often need replacement sooner. Our drivers flag visible warning signs during every delivery at no charge.

**What is the safe minimum oil level?**
We recommend never letting a tank drop below 20%. Below that level, sediment at the tank bottom can be drawn into fuel lines and clog the furnace. Running completely out of oil can introduce air into the fuel lines, requiring a technician to bleed the system before the furnace will restart.

**Is it normal for my tank to make noise?**
A humming or gurgling sound during delivery is normal. A whistling sound from the vent pipe can indicate a blocked vent alarm whistle, which should be inspected before the next delivery.

**Can I switch from oil to a different heating fuel?**
Yes. Contact us to schedule a consultation. Tank decommissioning (draining, cleaning, and either removal or fill-in-place) is available as a separate service.

**What should I do if I smell oil indoors?**
Ventilate the area, avoid open flames or switches near the smell, and call us immediately. Do not attempt to inspect the tank or lines yourself.

**How often should my tank be inspected?**
We recommend a professional inspection every 2 years for aboveground tanks and annually for underground tanks, in addition to the visual check performed at every delivery.
