# Service Area Coverage

Progress Home Heating Oil delivers to residential customers within a 45-mile radius of our depot. Our core service area is divided into five delivery zones, each run on a fixed weekly route day plus on-demand emergency dispatch.

## Delivery Zones
- **Zone 1 - North County**: Monday standard route
- **Zone 2 - Riverside**: Tuesday standard route
- **Zone 3 - Downtown & Suburbs**: Wednesday standard route
- **Zone 4 - South County**: Thursday standard route
- **Zone 5 - Outer Ring (30-45 miles)**: Friday standard route, will-call only, 150-gallon minimum order

## Outside the Service Area
We do not currently deliver beyond 45 miles from the depot. Customers just outside the boundary should contact us directly — we periodically expand coverage based on demand density and may be able to accommodate a route extension.

## New Customer Setup
New customers within the service area can be onboarded same-week in most cases. A brief phone or on-site tank assessment (size, age, fill pipe location, access) is required before the first delivery is scheduled.
