# Pricing & Plans

Progress Home Heating Oil offers three ways to pay for heating oil.

## Will-Call
Order oil whenever you need it at the current daily market price. No commitment, no fees. Best for customers who want full control over timing and price shopping. Minimum order is 100 gallons.

## Automatic Delivery
We track your estimated usage based on tank size, degree days, and delivery history, and schedule deliveries automatically so you never run low. Priced at the daily market price plus a $0.10/gallon service fee. Includes priority scheduling during winter storms and one free tank safety inspection per year.

## Budget Plan
Spreads your estimated annual heating oil cost into 11 equal monthly payments (July through May), with a final reconciliation in June. Available to Automatic Delivery customers with at least one year of delivery history. No interest or enrollment fee.

## Tiered Volume Pricing
| Tier | Gallons per Delivery | Price Adjustment |
|------|----------------------|-------------------|
| Standard | 100-199 | Market price |
| Volume | 200-349 | Market price - $0.03/gal |
| Bulk | 350+ | Market price - $0.06/gal |

## Additional Fees
- Emergency same-day delivery: $50 flat fee
- Extra delivery hose (over 100 ft): $15
- Declined payment: $45
- Tank safety inspection (outside annual free visit): $85
