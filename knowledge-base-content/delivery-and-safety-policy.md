# Delivery & Safety Policy

Progress Home Heating Oil delivers residential heating oil throughout our service area on scheduled and will-call routes.

## Delivery Windows
Standard deliveries are scheduled Monday through Friday, 7 AM to 6 PM. Customers receive a delivery date when an order is scheduled; specific delivery times are not guaranteed due to route variability. Same-day emergency deliveries are available for tanks below 15% capacity, subject to driver availability.

## Access Requirements
The delivery driveway and fill pipe must be clear of snow, ice, vehicles, and obstructions. Fill pipes must be accessible within 100 feet of the truck's parking location; a additional-hose fee applies beyond that distance. Locked gates must be left unlocked or a gate code provided in advance.

## Tank Safety Checks
Before every delivery, drivers visually inspect the tank, fill pipe, and vent pipe for damage, corrosion, or leaks. If a hazard is found, the delivery is paused and the customer is notified before oil is dispensed. Tanks more than 30 years old or without a working gauge require an inspection before the first delivery.

## Weather Delays
Deliveries may be delayed during severe winter storms for driver safety. Customers below 20% tank capacity during a forecasted storm are prioritized on the route.

## Payment
Payment is due upon delivery for will-call customers. Automatic delivery customers on a payment plan are billed monthly. A $45 fee applies to declined payments.
