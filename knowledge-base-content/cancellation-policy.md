# Cancellation & Order Change Policy

## Cancelling a Scheduled Delivery
Deliveries can be cancelled or rescheduled at no charge up until the day before the scheduled delivery date. Same-day cancellations are subject to a $25 dispatch fee if the driver has already been routed.

## Cancelling Automatic Delivery Enrollment
Automatic Delivery can be cancelled at any time with no penalty. Any remaining Budget Plan balance becomes due at final reconciliation, or can be spread across remaining will-call purchases at the customer's request.

## Switching Providers
Customers switching to another heating oil provider should notify us at least 5 business days before their next scheduled delivery to avoid an unwanted delivery. A final account statement is issued within 10 business days of cancellation.

## Refunds
Overpayments and Budget Plan credit balances are refunded within 15 business days of account closure, or applied to a final invoice if the customer remains active.

## Moving
Customers moving within our service area can transfer their account and delivery history to the new address at no charge. Customers moving outside the service area should schedule a final delivery and close their account; unused prepaid balances are refunded.
